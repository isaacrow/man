About Kitāb al-Shifāʾ WebAR

How to Use:

  • AR Camera Mode: Point your mobile camera (iOS Safari or Android Chrome) or desktop webcam at the illuminated manuscript image.
  • Desktop Simulator: Click the "AR Camera / Simulator" button at the top to orbit and inspect the manuscript in a virtual 3D environment.
  • Hotspot Pins: Tap the 3D glowing pins floating above the unwan header, calligraphy, and seal to read detailed codicological notes.
  • Live RDF Editor: Modify the Turtle RDF in Tab 4 to dynamically update the 3D hologram in real-time.
