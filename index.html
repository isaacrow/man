About Alqami

Alqami provides real-time augmented reality intelligence for historical manuscripts driven by semantic RDF graphs.

  • Camera Mode: Point your device at the manuscript to inspect 3D spatial metadata and features.
  • 3D Simulator: Switch modes to preview and rotate the manuscript in a virtual space.
  • Hotspot Anchors: Tap any 3D marker to inspect codicological notes.
  • Semantic Graph: View Dublin Core, CIDOC-CRM, and Schema.org relations in real-time.

Built with WebAR, Three.js, N3.js, and RDF Ontologies.
